VoiceMorph

A real voice changer for Android with two built-in voices - male and female - plus a live monitor and a game soundboard that works with PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, COD Mobile and friends.

Studio

Record a take, morph it to male or female with an adjustable strength slider, preview instantly and save clips to your library. Real DSP: WSOLA pitch shifting plus shelf-EQ timbre tilt - not a cartoon chipmunk filter.

Live

Real-time morphing with ~90 ms block latency: your mic is processed chunk by chunk and played back as the other voice. Perfect for monitoring, prank calls to yourself and speaker broadcast.

Game mode

A clip soundboard with a 3-second countdown and max-volume speaker broadcast, so the microphone of the game you switched to picks up your morphed voice.

Cloud presets

This page is the backend: GET /api/presets and POST /api/presets serve community voice recipes straight into the app.

Honest note about "in-game" voice changing. Android gives the microphone to exactly one app at a time - the game in front - and Android has no virtual-microphone API. Without root, no app can inject a changed voice into PUBG's voice chat in real time; apps claiming otherwise are fake or use the speaker trick. VoiceMorph does the speaker trick properly: record & morph in Studio, join voice chat, then Broadcast the clip at max volume - the game mic hears your new voice. On rooted devices a system-level audio hook would be required, which also risks game bans, so we deliberately do not do that.

Install

Download the APK, allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, tap the file, done. Android 7.0+ (API 24+), no account, no ads, ~250 KB.
